# Vision

## Why Seneschal Exists

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming integrated into enterprise software, automation platforms, and operational workflows. Organizations are beginning to deploy AI agents capable of interacting with internal services, infrastructure, data stores, and external systems.

While these agents increase productivity, they also introduce a new governance challenge.

Organizations often cannot answer fundamental questions such as:

- What capabilities does each AI agent possess?
- Which systems can an agent access?
- What actions are agents authorized to perform?
- Who approved those permissions?
- What has changed since the last audit?
- Why was a specific decision allowed or denied?

Traditional security platforms were designed around users, applications, and infrastructure—not autonomous AI systems capable of making decisions and invoking actions.

Seneschal exists to provide governance, policy enforcement, and observability for AI capabilities.

---

# Vision Statement

Seneschal enables organizations to discover, govern, monitor, and audit AI capabilities across every framework, runtime, and deployment model.

Rather than replacing existing AI frameworks, Seneschal provides the governance layer that sits between AI systems and enterprise resources.

---

# Mission

Provide organizations with confidence that AI systems operate within defined policies, are fully observable, and remain auditable throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Governance First

Every capability should be understood before it is trusted.

---

## Vendor Neutral

Organizations should not need different governance solutions for different AI frameworks.

Seneschal should integrate with existing ecosystems rather than replace them.

---

## Policy Before Execution

Actions should be evaluated before they occur—not merely logged afterward.

Organizations should be able to choose between monitoring, approval workflows, or enforcement depending on their risk tolerance.

---

## Complete Auditability

Every significant AI decision should be traceable.

Organizations should be able to answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- Which policy evaluated it?
- Who authorized it?
- When did it change?

---

## Simplicity

Governance should not require organizations to redesign their applications.

Policies should be understandable, transparent, and easy to manage.

---

# Long-Term Vision

As AI adoption accelerates, governance will become a foundational requirement rather than an optional feature.

Seneschal aims to become the standard governance platform for AI capabilities, providing a consistent model for discovery, authorization, policy evaluation, and audit regardless of the underlying AI technology.
